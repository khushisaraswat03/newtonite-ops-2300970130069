How priority works 🚩

  • P1 4 hours: something is down or many customers are hit
  • P2 24 hours: serious, but there's a workaround
  • P3 3 days: normal work
  • P4 5 days: nice to fix

The SLA clock starts when the team receives the item. The team's Lead can adjust priority later (with a reason).
